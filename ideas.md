# Harbor design direction

## Chosen direction: Tidal Ledger

**Commit: Tidal Ledger.**

## Design movement

Scandinavian administrative modernism + West Coast climate-tech restraint. No skeuomorphism, no 2004 enterprise chrome, no purple-AI gradient.

## Core principles

1. The manager is the product; People Ops is the buyer.
2. Clicks are classified as defects.
3. Payday is a feature.
4. One worker, many engagement types — never clone a person into modules.
5. Harbor is the ledger. The UI should feel like turning pages of one book, not hopping products.

## Color philosophy

Warm paper field, carbon ink text, a single harbor teal for action and brand. Amber for money-in-motion, clay for risk. Color is scarce so status is readable at a glance.

- Paper: `#F6F3EE`
- Ink: `#1C1915`
- Harbor teal: `#0F6E6B`
- Teal deep: `#0A4F4D`
- Foam: `#E7F3F2`
- Amber: `#C9842A`
- Clay: `#B4533A`
- Line: `#E6E0D6`

## Layout paradigm

Fixed left rail on desktop for People Ops / Finance / Supplier. Manager is a wide conversation column. Worker is a phone-width sheet centered on paper. Persona switcher is a persistent top bar, always in view for a live demo.

## Signature elements

- Harbor mark: a square teal field with a negative-space inlet (a “harbor gap”) cut from the right edge, and a thin ledger notch at the bottom — safe harbor + books.
- Request composer that looks like a Slack message, not a 40-field form.
- “Presenting as” banner under the switcher so a customer demo never loses the seat.
- Status pills: Draft, In review, Working, Time due, Payday, Paid, Flagged.

## Interaction philosophy

Optimistic, immediate, explainable. AI drafts sit as cards the human confirms. Switching persona never logs out and never resets the ledger. Creating a request as Priya must appear for Maya and Jordan in the same tab.

## Animation

Almost none. 120ms fades on cards. No mascots, no confetti on payday — a calm “Paid Friday” state is the celebration.

## Typography

- Display / product: **Fraunces** (old-style, a little maritime)
- UI / body: **Source Sans 3**
- Figures / money: **Source Sans 3** tabular lining

## Brand essence

The record you finish implementing. Safe harbor for the people who work for you and are not on payroll.

## Brand voice

Direct, specific, unimpressed by jargon. We say “paid on Friday,” never “AI-powered end-to-end talent orchestration.”

## Wordmark / logo

Wordmark is “Harbor” in Fraunces, medium weight, ink. Logo is the teal square mark with no letters. Favicon is the same mark. Do not put text in the icon.

## Signature brand color

Harbor teal `#0F6E6B` on paper `#F6F3EE`.
